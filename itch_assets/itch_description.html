NO MANA. ONLY GEARS, RPM & HEAT.

GEARBOX DUNGEON is a first-person 3D roguelike deckbuilder built for Game Off 2026 (theme: SHIFT). Pilot an armored vehicle from a diegetic 3D cockpit through an 11-sector mechanical megadungeon.

How It Plays

  • Shift Gears (R • N • 1st–6th): Every card requires a gear range, changes your RPM (0–8,000), and builds Heat. Every gear has its own passive.
  • Rev-Match & Gear Grind: You get 1 free +/-1 shift per turn. Hit a gear's optimal RPM band for bonus Shield, or force extra shifts to deal bonus damage at the cost of Heat & Hull.
  • Redline, Overheat & Stall: Push into Redline for extra damage, cool down in Neutral, or burn a card with [X] for +1,000 RPM to avoid stalling.
  • 3 Playable Chassis & 11 Sectors: Drive the Interceptor V8, Armored Bulldozer, or Sequential Prototype across 37 cards, 13 relics, 18 enemy types, and 3 bosses — with 100% procedural engine audio.

Controls

  • Shift Up / Down: [E] / [Q] or [Right/Left Shift] (or drag the 3D shifter)
  • Play Card: Drag above the dash (or onto an enemy), or [1–9] + [ENTER]
  • Burn Card (+1K RPM): [X]  •  Consumables: [Z] [C] [V]  •  End Turn: [SPACE]
  • Steer Fork: [1] / [2]  •  Sat-Nav Map: [TAB]  •  Guide: [H]  •  Pause: [P] / [ESC]  •  Fullscreen: [F]

Web Tip: Click the game canvas to focus keyboard controls and press [F] for Fullscreen. Built with Godot 4.5.
